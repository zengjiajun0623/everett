Fig. 1 · Lineage

Not a fork of the chain. A fork of the lesson.

Every previous "Ethereum alternative" copied the state: balances, contracts, history. ETC kept the unforked DAO-era ledger; ETHW kept the pre-merge ledger. ETHW inherited a corpse (bridged stablecoins worth zero, broken DeFi) and both inherited a contested identity. Everett inherits the code and the lessons and nothing else. Empty genesis. Zero balances. Every coin that will ever exist gets mined under rules fixed on day one.

Fig. 2 · The mechanism

One structural difference decides everything: is the payee in the room?

Under PoW, Ethereum's social layer cut miner pay twice (5→3→2 ETH) and shipped EIP-1559 over miner objections. Miners had hashpower; they had no vote. The Merge moved the security payees inside the room that votes, and in August 2026, EIP-8363 (an issuance cut co-authored by EF researchers) died in 72 hours. Same machinery, opposite outcome. The only variable that changed is drawn below.

Fig. 3 · Monetary design

Three answers to "who sets the security budget, and until when?"

Bitcoin fixed its schedule forever but lets it decay to zero: the guards eventually go unpaid unless fees save them. Ethereum keeps the budget adjustable forever, and the adjusters are the payees. Everett takes Bitcoin's fixedness and repairs its endgame: a decaying curve that lands on a permanent floor, so issuance-as-a-percentage falls toward zero while the absolute budget never does.

Bitcoin's steps exit the bottom of the chart and, in 2140, reach exactly zero. Ethereum's band is not a curve but a policy: it sits wherever its stakers vote it, which since 2024 has meant nowhere lower.

Known exposure

What the constitution buys, and what it costs

Rentable hashpower

Ethash is the most liquid rental market in PoW. ETC took three rental 51% attacks in one month of 2020, and the constitution forbids checkpoints. Survival plan is social: deep confirmations, monitoring, time.

Cold start

Fresh genesis means zero users, capital, and apps on day one. ETHW died launching into calm. Everett is built as a launch-in-waiting: worth little today, worth everything the week Ethereum's governance visibly fails.

Unfunded maintenance

Article V bans the treasury that would pay for perpetual client work. Every upstream CVE must be ported by volunteers or patrons, forever. Bitcoin's sponsor model is the bet.

Founder gravity

The constitution forbids economic privilege but cannot forbid personal authority. Whoever publishes it becomes its court of appeal. Satoshi solved this by vanishing. This project inherits that assignment.
